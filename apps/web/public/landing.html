Privacy isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

Your documents stay yours.

Most apps upload everything to their servers. VaultMind is local-first: your documents, the text we read from them, and your reminders live on your device — encrypted.

On-device by default

Browsing, search, categorisation and reminders work fully offline. We can’t read your documents.

You hold the keys

AES-256 encryption with a recovery phrase only you know. Optional encrypted backup we can’t open.

Consent before anything leaves

Cloud features are opt-in, per document, and clearly explained — never silent.

Built for the NDPA

Export or delete your data anytime, right from the app. Designed around Nigeria’s 2023 data-protection law.
